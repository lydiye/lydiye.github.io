Colors in Music

Welcome to my website, Colors in Music! I hope you have fun exploring all the colors that accompany great music. I've taken it upon myself to gather some of my favorite artists and their colorful album covers here to create a rainbow of my very own.

Click on "The Color Spectrum" in the navigation bar to see the full spectrum of colors and their corresponding albums. You can click on any cover to give an album a listen on Spotify and decide for yourself if it matches the color palette.

If you ever get the chance, you should create a spectrum of your own! (You can just make it a playlist, though. Making a website is pretty tough.)
